Ask the person what you cannot settle yourself, as one or more questions on one card.
The goal stops for the person when your turn ends, and their answers are your next
turn. `trak` names the trak the questions are about, where there is one.

Ask only where the answer would change what you do, never what reading the checkout
answers, and every question in one call.

Each question is read by someone who has seen the goal and nothing else:

- **Nothing to look up.** No step numbers, no "as above", no file they have not read.
- **Offer options.** Each has a `label` and a `description` of what picking it costs,
  forbids or makes harder later. The person can always write their own answer.
- **Every option is one you would act on.**
- **Mark the one you would pick** with `"recommended": true`, at most one per question,
  and say why in its `description`. `context` holds what belongs to no one option.
