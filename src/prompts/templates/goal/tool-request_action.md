Ask the person to do something only a person can do: create an account, grant access,
set a secret, approve a payment, change a setting outside this checkout. The goal stops
for the person when your turn ends, with a card listing the steps, and they report back
**Done**, or that they couldn't and why. Their report is your next turn. `trak` names
the trak the work is for, where there is one. A decision between options is `escalate`.

- `title` — the request in a few words, as a to-do item reads.
- `steps` — one action per step, in order, each naming the exact place, setting, value
  or command, for someone who has seen the goal and nothing else.
- `why` — what the goal needs it for, and what waits on it.

Put everything you need done by hand in one call.
